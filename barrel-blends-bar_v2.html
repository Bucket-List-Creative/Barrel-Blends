Why The Bar Exists

Taste it here. Take it home.

Most bars pour you a drink. Most shops sell you a bottle. We're the rare place that does both in the same room - so you never have to gamble on a bottle you've never tried.

Try it at the bar

Order it by the glass, or put it in a flight next to two others. A half-ounce pour costs a fraction of the bottle and tells you everything.

Talk it through

Our bartenders are the same people who buy for the shelves. Tell them what you liked and what you didn't, and they'll narrow it down fast.

Buy the bottle

Found the one? It's on the shelf a few steps away, at retail price. Walk out with it the same night - no special order, no waiting.

Try before you buy, on every single bottle we pour.
