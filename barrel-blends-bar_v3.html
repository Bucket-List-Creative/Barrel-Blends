The Room

A neighborhood bar
inside the bottle shop.

Meet friends for a round of craft cocktails. Split a whiskey flight. Grab a draft while you shop. Our bartenders pour what they're excited about, and they're happy to point you toward the shelf when you find your new favorite.

No reservation, no dress code, no minimum. Just walk in. Each of our three bars keeps its own rotating selection, so there's something a little different every time you visit.

Behind the Bar

What We Pour

Cocktails, wine by the glass, spirit pours and draft beer. Every selection rotates, and every bar keeps its own - so ask what just landed.

Cocktails

Cocktails

Classics made properly, alongside a rotating seasonal list our bartenders build around whatever they're most excited about right now.

Wine
Always changing

Wine by the Glass

Small, family-owned producers who let the fruit speak for itself. The pour list turns over often, so there's usually something new open.

Spirits
Whiskey · Agave · Rum

Spirit Pours

A deep, well-travelled back bar of whiskey, agave and rum. Tell us what you usually reach for and we'll point you somewhere worth trying.

Draft
Handles turn over constantly

Draft Beer

Crisp lagers through barrel-aged stouts, with the taps rotating as fast as we can find something worth putting on them.

The Whole Point

Discover something new
at our Bar.

Each Barrel Blends bar has its own rotating selection. Explore a new wine, whiskey, agave spirit or draft, talk with our team about what you enjoy, and discover something new along the way.

Tell us what you like

The people behind the bar are the same people who buy for the shelves. Name a bottle you love, or a style you keep coming back to, and they'll take it from there.

Try something you haven't

There's always something newly opened, freshly tapped or just landed. Order it by the glass and see whether it's your kind of thing.

Find your next favorite

When something clicks, there's a good chance it's on the shelf a few steps away - so you can walk out with it the same night.
